# Business Recommendations

Generated by the recommendation engine from the analysis artifacts (`DATA → ANALYSIS → INSIGHT → RECOMMENDATION`). Priority: **P0** = act now, **P1** = next quarter, **P2** = roadmap.

## 1. [P0] Launch a targeted retention programme for high-value At Risk customers

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** Champions + Loyal drive 80.4% of revenue; the At Risk segment alone holds £1,596,108 of historical revenue; the churn model flags 2,495 High-risk customers carrying £1,528,568 (9.3% of known-customer revenue), model AUC 0.7697.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Score all customers monthly with the trained model, prioritise High-risk customers with monetary value above the median, and run a win-back journey (personal outreach + incentives sized below the gross margin of the saved revenue).
- **Expected impact:** Saving 10% of High-risk revenue protects ~£152,857 per cycle.
- **Metric to track:** High-risk customer 90-day retention rate; revenue saved vs control group

## 2. [P0] Fix revenue realization: root-cause the 39 return hotspots and rising refunds

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** Realization fell from 96.86% (2009) to 91.66% (2011); every 1 percentage point of realization = £204,763; 39 SKUs exceed 10% refund rate; worst: PAPER CRAFT , LITTLE BIRDIE (100.0% refund rate, £168,470 refunded).
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Analyse root causes for the top hotspots (product quality, description mismatch, damages in transit), and block repeat refund patterns at order entry; add refund rate to supplier/product scorecards.
- **Expected impact:** Halving the hotspot refunds recovers ~£102,381 annually.
- **Metric to track:** Monthly realization %; hotspot refund rate

## 3. [P1] Convert guest checkout into identified, addressable customers

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** 22.9% of order lines are guest lines with no Customer ID (15.2% of revenue) — invisible to segmentation, churn and retention analytics.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Require or incentivise account creation at checkout (quote/reorder benefits for trade buyers), retro-tag existing guest lines where possible.
- **Expected impact:** Identifying half of guest revenue (£1,440,326) makes it actionable for retention and personalisation.
- **Metric to track:** Guest revenue share %; registered-customer count

## 4. [P1] Set discount guardrails — protect wholesale volume deals, cut value-destroying deep discounts

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** 35.2% of revenue sells below typical price at average depth 31.4%; deep-discount revenue £1,224,001 (6.4% of net revenue). High-discount customers churn LESS (40.5% vs 53.0%) and order much larger baskets — volume pricing works; uncontrolled deep discounts need approval thresholds.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Introduce discount approval tiers (e.g. >25% requires sign-off), monitor price realization per category, and keep negotiated volume discounts for high-frequency buyers.
- **Expected impact:** Capturing 20% of deep-discount revenue at full price adds ~£244,800 without volume loss (to be validated with an A/B test).
- **Metric to track:** Deep-discount share of revenue; realization % by category

## 5. [P1] Plan capacity, stock and cash against the seasonal forecast

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** Backtest: Seasonal Naive at 6.08% WMAPE; next quarter forecast £1,802,199 (95% range £1,287,952–£2,316,446); peak month 2011-11 = £1,456,146.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Use the quarterly forecast for staffing, purchasing and cash planning; run a pre-peak readiness review 8 weeks before the Q4 peak; refresh forecasts monthly as new data lands.
- **Expected impact:** Fewer stockouts in peak, lower emergency freight, better working-capital timing.
- **Metric to track:** Forecast WMAPE vs actual; peak-period fulfilment SLA

## 6. [P1] Rationalise the long-tail assortment and release working capital

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** 1,628 of 4,793 SKUs had no sale in 90+ days (prior-year revenue £1,685,339), while 2,168 SKUs show ≥8 zero-sales weeks at ≥1 unit/week average velocity — capital is parked in the wrong SKUs while demand-facing lines go unfulfilled.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Run a slow/dead-stock clearance (bundles, markdown with approval), stop reordering the tail, and apply demand-based reorder points from the inventory_features outputs for fast movers.
- **Expected impact:** Releasing even 10% of dead-stock-related stock value funds high-velocity lines.
- **Metric to track:** Dead-stock SKU count & value; stockout weeks on fast movers

## 7. [P2] Reduce UK concentration by scaling the strongest international markets

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** The UK is 85.0% of revenue; like-for-like growth is only 1.8%. Germany, France and Ireland are the largest non-UK markets (see net_revenue_by_region_country SQL output).
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Deepen the top-3 non-UK markets with localised campaigns/fulfilment; treat country-level cohort retention as the scorecard.
- **Expected impact:** Each point of non-UK mix shift diversifies ~£190k of revenue base (1pp of net revenue).
- **Metric to track:** Non-UK revenue share; international cohort retention

## 8. [P2] Tighten the product taxonomy to unlock category-level decisions

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** 'Other Products' holds 40.6% of net revenue — a catch-all bucket that makes category strategy, assortment and pricing decisions unreliable.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Reclassify the catch-all with a description keyword + fuzzy-matching pass, and require category assignment for new SKUs.
- **Expected impact:** Sharper ABC/discount/return analytics per category; cleaner Power BI category pages.
- **Metric to track:** Unclassified revenue share %

## 9. [P2] Industrialise the pipeline: monthly refresh, monitoring and model governance

- **Evidence (DATA/ANALYSIS):** Pipeline currently processes 1,067,371 rows end-to-end (clean → PostgreSQL star schema → 22 SQL analyses → ML models → dashboards) with row-level reconciliation (Python = PostgreSQL to the penny).
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Schedule monthly data refresh, track KPI drift (realization, churn rate, forecast WMAPE), retrain the churn model quarterly, and revalidate segment definitions twice a year.
- **Expected impact:** Decisions stay current; models remain trustworthy.
- **Metric to track:** Pipeline freshness; model AUC drift; KPI drift alerts
